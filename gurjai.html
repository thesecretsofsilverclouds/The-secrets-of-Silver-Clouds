Gurjai of Ten Thousand Hands

Gurjai is the heir to the Mantle of the Many and a prodigy known for keeping his hands in his pockets.

Explore Mantle of the Many

The pause between movements

Born in Lotus Peak, Gurjai is a prodigy already progressing towards Infinite Hands. To ordinary humans, he appears to be a young boy.

His speed once put his fellow monks in danger during training. Hisham taught him to return his hands to his pockets before each new movement or attack, creating a deliberate pause. Gurjai still uses the practice against enemies. The pause gives him a way to exercise restraint even when he could move faster.
