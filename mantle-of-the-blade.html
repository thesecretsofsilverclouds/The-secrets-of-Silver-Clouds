Codex · Martial tradition

Mantle of the Blade

The Mantle of the Blade is a martial tradition from Lotus Peak, rooted in swordsmanship and samurai-inspired discipline. The Crimson Lotus are one group within it.

Swordsmanship in a changing culture

Warriors from Lotus Peak brought their inherited martial traditions into contact with London street culture. Across generations, hoodies and trainers became part of their dress, and a broad-brimmed hat became a baseball cap with a 360-degree peak. The discipline of swordsmanship endured as the clothes changed.

The Crimson Lotus make that exchange visible in their swords, wide peaks and shared colours. Mercy shares their cultural roots and takes the style in her own direction.
