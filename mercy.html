Codex · Character

Mercy

Mercy is a sharp-tongued swordswoman from Lotus Peak. She takes her training seriously and has little patience for anyone who expects her to fall in line.

Mercy on her own terms

“Mercy’s a name, darlin. Not a promise.”

Mercy has a dry sense of humour and a knack for using it when someone tries to pull rank. She shares her martial roots with the Crimson Lotus and wears that heritage her own way, with pink-and-black punk streetwear and a wide-peak cap turned backwards.

Through the Sanctuary to London

Mercy followed the Mantle of the Blade out of Lotus Peak and through a portal into the human realm. They emerged in the Sanctuary in the Sky, the floating nightclub above London.

The Mantle clashed with local factions at first, then found its place in the city. Mercy, meanwhile, kept getting into trouble with MI6.

After Book One · Story spoilers

Just after the events of Book One, Mercy joined The Order under Shonen Kartia.
