Codex · Character

Mercy

Mercy shares Lotus Peak’s martial heritage with Riku, Kenzo and Rei. Her pink-and-black streetwear and backwards wide-peak cap give that heritage a distinct, punk-influenced expression.

Her own take on the tradition

Mercy comes from the same cultural roots as the Crimson Lotus. Her concept art keeps the sword and broad peak associated with the Mantle of the Blade, pairing them with pink-and-black streetwear, punk styling and a cap worn backwards.

Lotus Peak’s traditions have already changed through contact with London. Mercy’s look takes that evolution further, putting her own identity at the centre of how she wears her heritage.
