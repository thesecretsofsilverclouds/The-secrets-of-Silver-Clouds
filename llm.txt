# The Secrets of Silver Clouds

> The official world of Silent Silver: British urban fantasy shaped by anime, street culture and music.

The Secrets of Silver Clouds includes a published novel, a free illustrated first episode, the World Codex, browser games and Worldstream, an ongoing fiction experiment. The main action RPG with Xtended Realities is in development. Comics and animation are ambitions for the future, not currently released adaptations.

## A few doors into the lore
- [Duskkin](https://thesecretsofsilverclouds.com/duskkin): Cybernetic vampires powered by mythical blood.
- [Sanctuary in the Sky](https://thesecretsofsilverclouds.com/sanctuary-in-the-sky): The floating nightclub above London.
- [Demon's Legion](https://thesecretsofsilverclouds.com/demons-legion): A gang turned band and found family.
- [Lintels](https://thesecretsofsilverclouds.com/lintels): Cloud-like creatures of the world.
- [Road Sprites](https://thesecretsofsilverclouds.com/road-sprites): Small city creatures finding treasure in discarded things.
