The Streamliner

Concept

Summary

London’s enchanted train takes the direct route—even through buildings. Inside, noise-cancelling charms make passing through solid walls feel like an ordinary journey home.

Description

Outside the window, a building is coming straight towards the carriage. The Streamliner passes through it, edges blurring into ghostly afterimages before the train becomes solid again. Spatial magic, directed through its control matrix, has made obstacles someone else’s problem.

Inside, arcane symbols glow around plush seats and noise-cancelling charms soften the city. Goaden listens to music with a small dragon asleep on his shoulder. The wonder of London’s transport is how quickly its passengers can become bored with the impossible.

Background

Sleek and elegant, the Streamliner represents the pinnacle of technomagic integration in modern London. Its lightweight alloy exterior is infused with magical properties that enable it to phase through solid matter—buildings, underground structures, and urban obstacles—making direct and uninterrupted travel paths possible. The train's surface shimmers with subtly etched arcane symbols, while inside, enchanted carriages offer expanded space, noise cancellation charms, and ergonomically designed seating that magically adjusts to individual preferences. More than transportation, the Streamliner symbolizes progress and innovation, a testament to the harmonious blend of magic and technology.

Abilities

  • Space Expansion Enchantments

  • Regenerative Braking Energy Recovery
  • Protective Wards During Phasing

Examples

  • Spacious interiors despite compact exterior
