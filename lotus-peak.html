Codex · Realm

Lotus Peak

A portal inside the Sanctuary in the Sky opens onto Lotus Peak, a realm with its own cultures and martial lineages. Two of its known traditions are the Mantle of the Blade and the Mantle of the Many.

Beyond the Sanctuary portal

The Sanctuary in the Sky floats above London. Inside the former Church retreat, an ornate archway opens onto a hub of enchanted portals. One of those portals leads to Lotus Peak, where entire cultures and martial traditions have grown on the far side of the threshold.

The Mantle of the Blade carries forward a tradition of swordsmanship. The Mantle of the Many is a monastic lineage whose masters move so quickly that a single motion can leave the impression of many hands.

Culture and fashion

When Lotus Peak warriors encountered London street culture, their clothes changed over generations. Oversized hoodies took on the loose silhouettes of robes, trainers replaced traditional footwear, and the broad samurai hat evolved into a baseball cap with a peak that wraps all the way around.

The Crimson Lotus carry this exchange into their dress, while Mercy gives the same heritage a more individual, punk-influenced expression.
