Codex · Martial group

Crimson Lotus

Riku, Kenzo and Rei are members of the Crimson Lotus, a group within Lotus Peak’s Mantle of the Blade. Their crimson accents, traditional swords and circular-brim caps give the trio a recognisable silhouette.

A shared style

The Mantle of the Blade grew from the martial heritage of Lotus Peak. For the Crimson Lotus, that heritage lives alongside layered streetwear, loose trousers, trainers and caps with a peak that wraps around the brim. Traditional blades and crimson sashes tie the looks together.

Riku wears his black hair tousled, Kenzo wears his in braids, and Rei wears her dark hair tied back. Each has a distinct silhouette within the group’s shared visual language.
