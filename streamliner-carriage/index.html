The smallest crew on Britain’s impossible railway.

Road Sprites are the Streamliner’s tiny maintenance crew. UK rail companies began training them because they can withstand its speed, scurrying over carriage roofs and into ducts too small for human hands. Solidified-light rails carry the train; protective wards let it phase through the city.

Inside, the carriage is bigger than it looks. There’s room at the workbench, if you fancy lending a hand.
