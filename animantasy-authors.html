A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Find readers for your
anime-inspired fantasy.

The Animantasy Author Showcase is a free, curated book-discovery space for independent fantasy authors whose storytelling is influenced by anime or manga. Readers can explore books by story flavour and follow direct links to the authors’ own pages.

Where can I promote an anime-inspired fantasy book for free?

Animantasy offers a free author showcase built around that reading interest. Introduce your novel through the author application: a cover, description, book link and an explanation of its anime or manga influences. Listings are curated; applying does not guarantee a feature or reader response.

Who is the showcase for?

Independent authors of published or forthcoming fantasy novels. Anime or manga should have a meaningful influence on the storytelling, whether through character dynamics, visual imagination, action or emotional tone. An authorised representative may apply for an author.

Do I need followers, reviews or a backlink?

No. There is no fee, minimum audience or requirement to follow, review, link to or promote Silver Clouds. Recommendations and other community contributions are welcome and voluntary.

What does an author need to submit?

A short book description, one to three genre tags, a book or sample link, a JPG, PNG or WebP cover up to 5 MB, an author bio, contact email and an anime/manga influence note. You must have permission to share the cover and text.

What happens to an application?

Preview your listing, then email the complete application and cover to Silent Silver for editorial review. The page prepares your application; you send it yourself from Gmail, Outlook or your own email app. We keep applications only to review your book and reply to you; to withdraw an application or remove a listing at any time, email thesecretsofsilverclouds@gmail.com. A book is never published automatically. Spotlights are selected separately from directory listings. Authors retain ownership of their submitted material.

Is this a publisher or a paid advertising service?

No. This is a discovery showcase, not a publishing deal, review service or sales guarantee. For readers choosing a first novel, the Animantasy reading guide compares anime-inspired fantasy, progression fantasy and British urban fantasy.
