Short introduction

About the universe

The Secrets of Silver Clouds is a fantasy universe created by Silent Silver. Set in a transformed London, it combines anime-inspired action, British street culture, music and expansive fantasy worldbuilding.

Its characters include Ashai Bennet, who carries the celestial pendant Thyia; Goaden Reeves, wielder of the blade Vega; and the Demon’s Legion, a gang-turned-band whose lives run through the city’s magical underworld.

The published novel sits alongside an illustrated first episode, a World Codex, browser games and Worldstream, an ongoing fiction experiment. A main action RPG is in development with Xtended Realities.

Creator biography

Silent Silver

Silent Silver is the author and creator of The Secrets of Silver Clouds, leading its writing, worldbuilding and creative direction. Born and raised in Slough, England, Silent began sharing the world online before its first book was published.

Silent calls the approach Animantasy: fantasy storytelling shaped by anime’s visual energy, expressive characters and dramatic action.

Using this site

Privacy & browser data

Your saved progress

The experiences use browser storage for your Holy Gift, game saves, reading preferences and Worldstream traveller profile. These local saves belong to the browser and site where you created them; they do not automatically follow you to another device. Clearing this site’s browser data can remove them.

Worldstream also connects to an online service. Reactions, comments and feedback you choose to submit are sent to that service. Shared faction Glory totals use Google Firebase.

Analytics & other services

The homepage and some other pages load Google Analytics to measure site use. Site fonts are served by Google Fonts. The contact form is provided by Google Forms and loads when you choose to open it.

Links to shops, social networks, community services and externally hosted games take you to services with their own privacy practices. For questions about this site or a message you have sent, contact thesecretsofsilverclouds@gmail.com.
