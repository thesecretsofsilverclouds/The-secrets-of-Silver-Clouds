Silver Wings

Concept Silver Solace

Summary

Silver Wings are ancient guardians of souls, with synthetic silver feathers and the power to open passages between dimensions. One stepping out of a nightclub through a slit in reality makes the worlds beyond ordinary life feel suddenly, unsettlingly close.

Description

Ashai almost collides with a Silver Wing in the Sanctuary. The stranger’s smoky robes, remote violet eyes and immense metallic wings make the surrounding party seem suddenly small. Then a slim opening splits the air, showing impossible vistas, and the being steps through.

Anarchy knows them as ancient Guardians of souls. Their role reaches beyond the human habit of calling anything winged an angel: Silver Wings carry out the passage between lives and realms. Meeting one is a reminder that the worlds beyond the next doorway do not all belong to the living.

Background

Androgynous. Beautiful. Terrifying in their perfection.

What it is

The Silver Wings hail from Silver Solace—a heavenly plane that exists beyond anything mortal minds can grasp. Wings shimmer silver and black, a millennia-old fusion of organic feathers and cybernetic implants woven so seamlessly that nature and technology become indistinguishable. Nano-engines embedded in their wings allow them to tear open wormholes, manipulating space and time like spiders weaving cosmic webs. Their synthetic skin, flawless and smooth, renders gender meaningless—they are not individuals but impartial arbiters, cosmic librarians sorting souls on the infinite shelves of the multiverse. They serve no deity.

How it works

Follow no doctrine. Operate on a scale so vast it makes empires look like dust motes. When someone dies, the Silver Wings appear. Not to judge sins or weigh virtue—moral judgment is too small, too human—but to assess the soul's resonance, its alignment with the various realms of the afterlife. Each soul is sorted, not condemned or saved, but catalogued and delivered to where it belongs in the grand tapestry.

Impact

Their arrival signals dimensional disturbances, shifts in reality's fabric. They appear at the towering Ascension Spires where souls are randomly assigned to a Silver Wing—fairness guaranteed through cosmic lottery. Then the wormholes open, and souls travel through the kaleidoscopic Rift between all realms. The Silver Wings remain eternal, guardians and pathways, beautiful and cold, watching over the transition between life and what comes after.
