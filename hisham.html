Hisham of Infinite Hands

An early master of Infinite Hands, Hisham taught Gurjai how to control a speed that could endanger those around him.

Restraint in practice

Hisham is Gurjai’s teacher and predecessor in the Mantle of the Many.

When Gurjai’s speed put other monks at risk during training, Hisham taught him to return his hands to his pockets before moving again. Giving each movement a deliberate pause helped Gurjai exercise control at a pace few others could follow.
