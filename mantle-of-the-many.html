Lotus Peak · Martial lineage

Mantle of the Many

In this monastic martial lineage from Lotus Peak, a master can move so fast that a single gesture appears as a multitude of hands.

Explore Lotus Peak

The discipline of speed

Even ordinary monks of the Mantle move considerably faster than ordinary humans. At the highest levels, that speed leaves visible afterimages, as though a practitioner had acted several times within a single instant.

The titles describe levels of mastery. Hisham of Infinite Hands is among the early practitioners to attain that level. His student Gurjai of Ten Thousand Hands is the current heir and is already approaching Infinite Hands. Nahla of Five Thousand Hands is a master whose precision and economy of movement set her apart.

The Mantle of the Many is one of the known lineages of Lotus Peak. The realm is accessible through a portal inside the Sanctuary in the Sky, high above London.
