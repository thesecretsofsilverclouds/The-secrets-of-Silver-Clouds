What I can help you make

Bring me in to write a scene, develop a setting your team can build on, or turn an early pitch into something people can play.

Game writing & branching dialogue
Story outlines, character arcs, scene scripts and in-world writing. I can develop material from a brief or write within your existing lore and character voices.
Worldbuilding & lore bibles
Character, faction and location guides, timelines and rules your team can use to keep a setting consistent across a game, novel or adaptation. Browse the Silver Clouds World Codex.
Creative technology & web prototyping
Playable browser prototypes, interactive readers and audio-led demos with timed visuals. A small proof of concept lets your team test how the audience explores, listens or plays.
Story adaptation & creative direction
Comic scene breakdowns, adaptation briefs and visual direction for animation or immersive audio. Watch an animation proof of concept.
