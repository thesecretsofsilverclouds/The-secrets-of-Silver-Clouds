Nahla of Five Thousand Hands

Nahla is a master of the Mantle of the Many whose movements are disciplined, precise and economical.

Precision at speed

Nahla holds the title of Five Thousand Hands in the Mantle of the Many. Even among monks trained to move at extraordinary speeds, she stands out for how little movement she needs.

Gurjai is known for his extraordinary speed. Nahla is distinguished by how precisely she controls each gesture.
